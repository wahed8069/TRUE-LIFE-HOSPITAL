Health Guidance FAQ

Common Patient Health Questions

Quick answers from our medical practitioners in Karulai.

When should I bring a feverish child or infant to the hospital?

If a child has a temperature above 101°F that persists for more than 48 hours, refusal to drink fluids, lethargy, or rapid breathing, you should visit our OPD or 24-hour casualty immediately for clinical evaluation and rapid blood testing.

Can I walk in late at night without an appointment?

Yes. True Life Hospital operates Open 24 Hours Daily. Our emergency reception, duty medical officers, and pharmacy on Govt. Hospital Road are active round the clock for any urgent symptoms, sudden abdominal pain, injuries, or acute fevers.

How often should adults monitor their blood sugar and blood pressure?

Adults above 35 years in Kerala should undergo baseline fasting blood sugar (FBS) and blood pressure checks at least once every 6 months. For patients diagnosed with diabetes or hypertension, monthly checkups and quarterly HbA1c tests ensure optimal health.
