Our Hospital Mission

Compassionate healthcare rooted in patient listening.

Centrally located at CK Tower on Govt. Hospital Road, Karulai, True Life Hospital was established to provide reliable, round-the-clock medical services where patients are treated with genuine respect and thorough medical attention.

We understand that visiting a doctor can be stressful, especially during late-night emergencies or unexpected illnesses. That is why our clinical philosophy is built on patient listening. Our physicians take the time to hear your concerns, perform thorough examinations, and explain diagnoses and treatments in clear Malayalam and English.
